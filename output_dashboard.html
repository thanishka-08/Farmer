Core Algorithms & Data Structures Implemented in Backend:

1. PriorityQueue<HarvestLot>:

Dynamically prioritizes lots based on Urgency (Levels 1–5 descending) and Delivery Deadline (hours ascending). Guarantees most urgent lots get allocated first.

2. Dijkstra's Algorithm (Weighted Graph):

Uses HashMap<String, HashMap<String, Double>> adjacency list to compute shortest travel times and reconstruct full node-by-node dispatch paths.

3. Polymorphism & Inheritance:

Abstract StorageCenter with specialized subclasses ColdStore and DryStore enforcing custom crop acceptance rules via acceptsCrop(crop).

4. Partial Allocation & Vehicle Capping:

If a lot quantity exceeds vehicle capacity or remaining market demand, the engine creates partial dispatches and cleanly tracks remaining quantities.
